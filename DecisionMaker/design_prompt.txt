You are an expert Streamlit developer. Build a complete, self-contained Streamlit MVP for a Decision Tracking & Analysis System.
Goal
The application helps companies track and analyze where decisions are actually being made by logging key decisions and using AI to monitor and analyze them.
Core Features
Sidebar Navigation

First option (default): Decision Logging
Second option: Reports & Analysis

1. Decision Logging Tab (Main View)

A clean, easy-to-use form that users fill out every time a decision is made:
Who made the decision (dropdown or text input + option to add new people)
What the decision was (textarea)
Why the decision was made (textarea)
Date/Time (auto-filled, editable)
Optional: Department / Area / Impact Level (Low/Medium/High)

Submit button to save the decision to the database.
AI Monitoring Section (on the same page or collapsible section):
Button: "Start AI Server Monitor" (simulated)
The AI should simulate monitoring company activity and automatically log decisions when it "detects" them.
Display a log of AI-detected decisions with the ability to review/edit before saving.


2. Reports & Analysis Tab

Display a summary dashboard of all logged decisions.
AI Analysis:
Button: "Run AI Analysis" — The AI analyzes all logged decisions to determine where decisions are actually being made (who makes the most, which departments, patterns, bottlenecks, etc.).
Generate insights and visualizations.

PDF Report Generation:
Button to generate a professional PDF report.
The report should include: decision logs, key metrics, AI insights, and visualizations.
PDF must be viewable in the app and downloadable.
